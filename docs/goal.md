## Objective

Make this account the strongest the game allows, and make the system that plays it good enough that nobody has to operate it: the bot, the collector with its coordinator, and the dashboard. I want to open the dashboard, see where the account stands and why, and never have to steer.

## Done when

1. Every character of data/roster.json is level 60 with the best gear the account can give it, every AA rank that makes it or its group stronger, and its class epic.
2. Every boss in data/raid-goal.json has been killed, with its kill lines and a screenshot in the diary.
3. The dashboard shows each of these as a measured bar and what moved it, and a first-time reader understands every panel.
4. The account plays a full day without a manual intervention and without an attention item left open.

How to get there is yours: the milestones, their order, the design and the experiments. Keep the plan and its measurements in the repository, so that a later session continues from them.
