# Boss kills

How the coordinator decides which bosses and named enemies the account kills, how, and when. A boss kill is one more way to get gear and quest pieces, valued in the currency of [gear acquisition](gear-acquisition.md), hours of a group's progress, so it compares with hunting, camps and visits.

## Problem

- The daily list's two free summons went unused every day until 2026-11-10, and the raids since were started by hand. A casino potion waits for a hand too, and the bot had no way to use one.
- The bosses also spawn in the open world, and nothing hunts them there.

## What is known, and how

Verified in the game or in the client's code:

- A group of six kills some bosses without raid mode: group 1 at levels 51 and 52 killed the open-world Lady Vox at 20:52 on 2026-11-10 in normal play, and Tagujazolu gained 88,539 XP (G1's game log).
- Raid kills pay the leader XP: Phinigel Autropos 390,451 per kill, Lady Vox 435,600 (G1's game log).
- A daily summon makes a private copy. The client's panel: "Two free resets per account every rolling 24 hours, with one kill per boss. Resuming a boss or retrying a wipe is free. Casino potions do not use resets. Private bosses last 24 hours; nearby enemies remain shared."
- An open-world kill sets the boss's daily cooldown too: after the 20:52 kill the daily list showed Lady Vox on cooldown until 20:52 the next day.
- Raid recruits fought while their own groups kept playing them (the raids of 2026-11-10).
- Raid time is bought by the hour, quoted at 8,000 pp with the highest character at 52 (`iq.raid.time()`).
- A casino potion is used with the client's consume function (`ds` in `Bip1bGL8.js`), which sends `casinoPotion: true`. The client's casino table names each boss's zone and spawn point.

Open-world spawns, from the server's content tables: respawn times of about 12 h for Phinigel Autropos and Lodizal, 72 h for Lady Vox, Lord Nagafen, Venril Sathir, Trakanon, Innoruuk, Cazic-Thule and Lord Yelinak, 168 h for Vulak`Aerr, spawn chance 0 for the four Warders, and no open-world spawn for Tunare and the Avatar of War. Seen live in the tabs' spawn lists on 2026-11-12: Phinigel Autropos alive at his point in Kedge Keep, Innoruuk respawning in the Plane of Hate. The server gives a zone's spawn list only to a character in that zone (a request for another zone answers "Your zone is out of sync. Resynchronize before loading enemies."), so a boss's state is known from the tabs that are in its zone.

A raid target stands apart from AutoLevel: the client marks it `autoLevelExcluded` in the spawn list, and it still attacks a party that fights inside its aggro radius. Phinigel Autropos joined 51 fights of the groups in Kedge Keep in a day, all of them pulls of the four swirlspine seahorses that spawn 111 to 163 units from him (aggro radius 200), and won all 52 fights with him since midnight of 2026-11-12. The bot keeps AutoLevel off the spawn points inside such an enemy's aggro radius (`guardedSpawns` in `src/bot.js`).

## Design

### Sources and ways

A boss kill has a source and a way. Sources: the open-world spawn on its respawn timer (shared with other players), a private copy from a free daily summon (two a day) or from a casino potion (its holder's). Ways: the boss's zone visited by one group in normal play, or a raid of all 72 characters in raid mode, which needs raid time. Each pair is a candidate, and the coordinator takes the cheapest way that kills.

### Kill chance

The raid simulator fights each candidate with the surveyed characters: the group's six for a group kill, all 72 for a raid. A background job refreshes the results after each gear survey and keeps them in the db meta key `raidOdds`, so a decision never waits for a fight. A candidate counts as killable when every simulated fight kills it. The first live kill of each kind checks the simulator, as group 1's Lady Vox did for a group of six. A live wipe holds the candidate until the next survey changes the roster, and the fight's trace names the cause.

### Value and cost

A kill is worth the XP it pays and the items of its loot table at their drop chances: each copy the account's demand still needs (`docs/plans/items.md`) and each gear upgrade at its `copyValue`. A group kill costs the group's trip and fight time against its hunting. A raid costs the leading group's time and its share of the raid hour, which covers every raid kill in it.

### Schedule

- Open-world spawns: a boss of the daily list or of the raid goal that a tab sees alive at its spawn point is killed there by a raid, without a summon, so its summon stays for another boss (`openBosses` in `tools/lib/boss-plan.mjs`, the db meta key `bossesSeen`). Each snapshot of a tab carries the raid targets of its zone's spawn list. The raid looks at the zone's list before it buys anything and ends when the boss no longer stands. A raid costs the fleet little: the groups' XP per minute fell by 0.4 and 0.6 group-hours over the 25 minutes around the raids of 2026-11-11, inside its usual swing.
- The watch: twelve of these bosses have a live spawn point (`watchedBosses`, from the content's spawn tables), ten of the daily list and Lord Yelinak and Vulak`Aerr, who are on no list, so the open world is the only place the account kills those two. Each is one kill more than the two free summons a day, worth 0.2 to 3.4 hours of a group's progress on 2026-11-12. Other players kill them too: of the seven the tabs had seen by then, six were down, each with the time it comes back, and Phinigel Autropos had stood for twelve hours. So a group looks (`nextLook`, the bot's `look` task): it travels to the boss's zone, reads the spawn list and goes home. A look goes to a boss the plan would kill if it stood, first to one whose respawn is due, ahead of it and staying past it (`LOOK`), then to one that stood more than half an hour ago, then to one no tab has seen. A look that finds its boss standing brings the coordinator's next pass at once, and the strongest group travels there to lead the raid, its hunt stopped for it. The sightings are kept (`bossLog`), and the dashboard's "Bosses in the open world" shows each boss, when it comes back and how long it stood before someone killed it. Open: how long that is, which the first respawns with a look there will show, the danger of a zone's entry to the group that looks, and whether the leader reaches a boss deep in its zone.
- A group or the raid: a group of six can fight a boss in normal play, through a hunt that picks it in Enemy Targets (`directedKill` in `src/bot.js`), at no cost and with XP for all six. Group 1 killed Lady Vox at 51 and 52 that way (88,539 XP for the leader, against 435,600 for the leader alone in the raid) and Queen Velazul Di`zok, level 62, at 55. The boss plan still sends the raid, because nothing judges a group against a boss with its company. The simulator fights a boss alone and gave group 1 four kills of four in 79 s against Phinigel Autropos. In two fights group 1 chose at level 56 on 2026-11-12, he kept 58% of his 18,000 HP over 182 s beside one swirlspine seahorse, and beside two the leader stood at 549 of 3,591 HP after 96 s. No heal landed on him. The seahorses and he hit for 73 and 167 HP a second, cast area damage (Word Divine, Inferno of Al'Kabor) and dispelled the group's buffs and heals 16 times in the 96 s. Open: simulate a boss together with the spawns inside its aggro radius, for a group and for the raid, and check it against these fights.
- What the simulator lacks for that, from a first try on 2026-11-12: handed a second enemy's CombatInit two seconds into a fight, it gave the same fights event for event, so the helper never fought. And it is off against Phinigel Autropos alone. There group 1 kills him in 71 s, takes about 3,000 damage and sees one cast begin. Live, the group dealt him 5,192 damage in 182 s, a ninth of the simulated rate, while he began 20 casts, among them roots and dispels, and the party took 13,256 damage. What makes the difference is not established. The simulated boss barely casts, the simulated scene keeps every member in melee range for the whole fight, so a root costs nothing there, and the group fights in raid mode on orders, not as a party by its tactics. That gap has to close before a boss's company matters. Until then the live record decides for a group (`recordCounts`).
- The raid against Cazic-Thule, 2026-11-12: the simulator kills him in four fights of four, and the raid lost three. Two of the gaps are closed: the live leader never fought while the bot kept 3D off, and the boss's area spells hit nobody in the simulator because its actors had no place. In the third fight, with the leader tanking, the boss dealt the raid 743 HP a second (268,372 melee and 143,114 from spells on 67 members in 554 s), the healers cast mostly Minor and Light Healing (1,152 of 1,613 heal casts), the clerics' mana was gone by 230 s, and his weapon's Panic and his Invoke Fear feared members. A simulated fight with the area spells takes 1,720 a second on all 72, heals 1,425 a second and kills in 402 s with 7 deaths. Still open: what the simulated healers do with their mana against the live ones, and what fear does to a raid member in the simulator. Until the simulator agrees with live fights, a boss the raid fought and lost to waits twelve hours (`waiting` in the boss pass of `tools/iqd.mjs`).
- Free summons: used every day on the killable bosses whose cooldown has passed, because an unused summon is lost. Of the goal's nine only Cazic-Thule is on the daily list. The simulator kills him with the raid of all 72 in four of four fights, in 589 s on average with 11.75 deaths a fight and the leader dead in every second one (2026-11-11, groups at 45 to 54).
- Order: a potion's boss that stands goes first, and every other kill goes by the worth of its loot per minute of raid time, the simulator's fight and three minutes of setup together (`nextBossKill`, `RAID_SETUP_MIN`). A boss of the raid goal that no raid has killed yet is planned whatever it drops and also outside paid time, but it takes its turn: a ten-minute fight comes after bosses that die in seconds and may fall to other players meanwhile.
- Potions: used in a raid by their holders.
- Raids on a summon or a potion run as soon as one is due, in the order above: a free summon comes back 24 hours after its use, so waiting for the second shifts the first. An hour of raid time costs 10,000 platinum, a third of what the account's sales bring in a day (30,241 platinum on 2026-11-11, when three hours were bought). A summon, a potion and a goal boss buy their hour. An open-world boss outside the goal is killed inside an hour that runs, or buys one while fewer than two hours ran in the last 24 (`OPEN_BUY`, the db meta key `raidHours`), which leaves the third for the summons, and only when the open-world kills of that hour are worth one hour of a group's progress together. That price is assumed: nothing measures what else the platinum would buy. The leader pays from its carried coin and pulls the account's largest balances until it holds the price: on 2026-11-12 the account had 49,000 platinum and no character over 9,300. A raid task runs for three times its simulated fight, between a quarter of an hour and 45 minutes, and longer while the boss still loses health and paid raid time lasts: Cazic-Thule's first raid ended at its 30 minutes about 75 s before the kill. When Run To Target leaves the leader out of reach, he walks to the boss with the ground navigation, since in that raid he stood 2,222 away and the boss killed 66 of 71 without its tank.
- A raid orders no slow and no debuff: in all four fights that ordered them the page stopped taking the server's messages at that second and the fight was lost (diary/2026-11-11-d.md).
