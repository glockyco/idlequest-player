# 2026-11-08 (early morning): auditing the project's assumptions

Several plans had rested on old readings, and on Domeyafun being treated as the default leader and the Magician as the default damage class. Seven agents audited the docs, the bot and tools, and a spell upkeep for every class. Every decision-relevant claim then got a verdict against the client code, the content snapshot, a measured run or the wiki. Each fix was checked before it was committed.

## What turned out wrong

- The XP model treated special abilities 22 and 23 as full melee immunity. The client reads 19 as melee immunity and 23 as immunity to non-magical hits only, and it ignores 22. Every weapon and pet in group 1 is magical, so Kaesora had been written off as melee-immune at about 1.0M XP per hour. The corrected model ranks it among the best zones from 31 to 40.
- The model also cut candidates at three levels below the leader. The bot fights everything that is not green, including enemies 4 to 8 levels below, and those pay best once HP outgrows XP from level 37. With the bot's band, Solusek's Eye leads from 32 to 40, ahead of Unrest, Kaesora and Splitpaw. The band rows moved into `data/band-rotations.json`, which the bot and the gear tool share.
- The roster plan sized raids from class templates that recruits never use. Recruits fight with their real combat profiles. A level-60 Magician pet deals about 11 to 20 DPS in a raid, not 62 to 90, and nukers run out of mana in 60 to 90 seconds. Groups 2 and 3 now add the eight missing classes (Paladin, Druid, Necromancer, Rogue, Shadow Knight, Wizard, Ranger, Monk) with one Magician in total. Groups 4 to 6 wait for the level-46 raid test.
- From level 34, the bot's spell upkeep would have memorized pet heals the leader never casts, because the client's automatic casting uses only its static table for each class. The upkeep now plans gems for every class from that table (`data/class-spells.json`, extracted by `tools/class-spells.mjs`). Warrior, Monk and Rogue leaders cast nothing, so they lose nothing to the 8-gem limit.
- The cleanup's explicit sales bypass Keep Epic Quest Items, so tradable epic pieces worth under 8 pp could have been sold. The bot now keeps the last copy of the 3,515 items that quest scripts accept in turn-ins.
- The bot would have fought raid mode: restarting AutoLevel, recalling the party and fleeing bosses eight levels up. It now stands aside while raid mode is active.
- Cazic-Thule does have an `npc_types` row and spawns at 100%, as do Yelinak and Vulak`Aerr. The four Warders and Lord Vyemm have spawn chance 0. The Warders, Tunare and the Avatar of War appear in the casino potion table. Vyemm has no script spawn and no casino entry, so his kill may not be possible in the current content.

## What held

- The kill-XP formula fits all 3,132 recorded leader kills to within half an XP point, and every zone visited so far has the same multiplier in the content snapshot and in the wiki. The analyzer now checks each new zone's multiplier from its kills (`--member` rates one companion, for setups with different leaders).
- Water stays the Magician pet line: all four elements hit the same in melee at every tier, and Water adds a damage spell and more HP than Fire or Air.
- Two sessions on the account were seen to stay connected, but an earlier attempt failed, so it gets tested again before group 2.
