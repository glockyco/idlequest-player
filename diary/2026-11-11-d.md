- I put the loss of the first Lodizal fight down to the page being hidden while play moved to its own terminal tab. The second fight was lost the same way with the page visible and the collector untouched.
- I reported Lodizal dead after the second fight on the task's word, without looking for the kill line. The spawn list and the missing XP showed otherwise within a minute.
- "The raid is 5580 away from Lodizal" is the distance from the zone's entry point to his spawn point. The leader never walked there in any of the fights, and the 71 members fought without him. It says nothing about how far he fled.

Four such kills on 2026-11-10 fit a factor 1.5 by chance within 94 seconds, in two tabs, and the log showed an event that never was. I first explained it as one tab counted in the wrong zone and wrote that into a comment before I looked at the kills. Only kills with six XP lines count now.
