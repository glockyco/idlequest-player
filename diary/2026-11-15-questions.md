Each question has the facts, the options and a recommendation. Mark the option you want, or write another.

## 1. Sell crafted items for coin?

Merchants pay an item's own price, and the crafting catalog sells components for far less.

| Craft | Components for one attempt | What comes out | Sale |
|---|---|---|---|
| Aqualung (Tinkering 148) | 22 platinum | 20 Aqualungs | 170 platinum each, measured on one |
| Powered Gloves (Tinkering 122) | 8 platinum | 1 | price 40 platinum, sale not measured |
| Rebreather (Tinkering 175) | 382 platinum | 1, at 68% now | price 800 platinum, sale not measured |

The Aqualung's 20 looks like a server error: in EverQuest's data that number is the item's charges, and here it comes out as 20 items. The other two give one item, as their recipes say.

The account earns about 178,000 platinum a day, and all AA ranks cost 10.4 million. The Tinkering training for the Warriors' Rebreathers left 2,046 Aqualungs (about 348,000 platinum at that price), 84 Powered Gloves and 76 Spyglasses in the Tradeskill Bank, and 12 Rebreathers more than the Warriors need.

- [ ] A. Sell nothing and craft nothing for coin.
- [ ] B. Sell what the training left except the Aqualungs, and craft nothing for coin.
- [x] C. As B, and let one tab craft items that come out one per attempt for coin, with a daily cap. Leave the Aqualungs unsold. (Recommended: crafting and selling is normal play, the Aqualung count is not, and coin is what limits the AA ranks.)
- [ ] D. As C, and sell and craft Aqualungs too.

## 3. Should tanks keep a shield?

Since `9ffc400` the gear plan weighs a two-hander against a main hand with a shield as sets. It takes the shield off a tank when the two-hander's damage is worth more than the shield's AC and HP by the survival weight, which is small because nobody dies in normal hunting: Ivelugias, who takes 72% of group 9's hits, got a two-hander for +2.6%.

- [x] A. Keep the plan's choice. (Recommended: leader deaths are 0.3% of the day's time.)
- [ ] B. A group's tank always keeps a shield.
