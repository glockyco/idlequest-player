# 2026-11-11 (night and morning): epic pieces as targets, weapon stats, generated chains, zone crowding, camps and the kill prior

## What happened

- Epic pieces became gear acquisition targets (step 3 of `docs/plans/epic-model.md`). A piece is worth its reward's gain to the character that lacks the epic, shared by the pieces it lacks, and only epics the account can finish have their pieces hunted.
  - A piece an enemy drops after a chain of scripted deaths comes from the static enemy that starts the chain. The black reaver of the City of Mist respawns itself on rolls 1 to 80 of its death and spawns Lord Rak`Ashiir otherwise, so every static spawn of the reaver ends in Lord Rak`Ashiir after five reaver kills on average. The Iksar Scale's target is the reaver with Lord Rak`Ashiir among the enemies its hunt kills, at his level 54.
  - A drop row now carries the kills one copy takes where that differs from one over the spawn and drop chances.
- The gear plan valued a weapon only by its swings. Its stats served as a guard against swapping away a stat weapon and never counted in the gain, so the Spear of Fate was worth nothing to a Shaman. A weapon now counts for its stats like armor, beside its swings.
  - That made quest pieces such as the Broom of Trilon look like weapons to the Magicians. The pieces the epics hand in now stay unworn, since a hand-in takes them from the bags.
- Epic chains are generated from the model's steps (step 2), split where a hunted piece comes in. The generated Shaman chain replaced `shaman-epic-spear`, and the Earthcaller got three parts.
- `bun tools/epic-model.mjs` now names the step kinds each epic lacks. Only the Shaman's and the Earthcaller's run on today's kinds. A coin hand-in would add the Paladin and the Necromancer, and a summoning cast the Shadow Knight and the Magician. A purchase from a merchant outside the hand-in's zone blocks seven epics.
- The client's Give Items window has an amount for each coin (`quest-give-currency-` in the bundle), so a coin hand-in needs only the bridge to fill it.
- Overnight the coordinator sent groups to the black reaver for the Iksar Scale. Group 1's visit at 01:56 fought reavers for its seven minutes and left mid-chain, and the four visits after it found no reaver up. A visit took one kill's time for a copy at the end of a chain of six kills on average. A visit now takes its chain's kills and pays the deaths of every fight in it (`chainKills`), so it runs 9 to 15 minutes by the group's kill rate. A visit that finds none of its enemies up names the first respawn of their spawn points (`watch` of `iqBot.hunt`), and the coordinator comes back then instead of after an hour.
- The dashboard showed most groups idle 10% to 26% of their last hour, nearly all of it for crowding. Nine groups at levels 40 to 43 shared the three zones of the band row from 38, and the model's crowd factors made sharing Solusek's Eye and Paw look worse than the groups measure it, so five to seven groups gathered in Kaesora. Three changes went in:
  - the crowd factors weigh each zone's measured shares against the model's (`crowdShare`, db meta `zoneCrowding`);
  - a group within two levels of the next band row also ranks that row's zones (`bandZones`);
  - a zone without measured time starts at the model's rate, scaled to the measured zones. The first pass with the next row's zones ranked the Tower of Frozen Shadow at 3.48M on the row's median, where the model gives 2.35M.
- The server restarted for a patch at about 07:55, and every tab was out of the world for about ten minutes. The supervisor signed the pages in again. Two pages entered the world from the character select without the zone state the client sends with a travel, so their hunts failed with "Your zone is not synchronized. Reconnect before travelling." The bridge now records such a refusal (`faults.desync`), and the supervisor reloads the page. Both pages healed before the rule was live, one by a character switch, so the reload has not run yet.
- A camp earns no XP. Over the last twelve hours the leaders earned 0.01M XP an hour in 17.3 hours of coordinator camps, against 2.73M in their normal play, and group 7 camped 5.9 of those hours. The acquisition model had priced a camp at the share of the group's XP its trip zone does not pay, so a camp in a zone as good as the group's own cost nothing. A camp now costs its whole time, like a visit, and the first pass after the change found every camp not worth it.
- The Shovel of Ponz hand-in at Vira failed for Mashidond at 06:52 and 08:20. The server refused the first trade ("The NPC does not want these items."), which it does when its view of the bags lags a pull. The quest step retries a refused trade after talking to the NPC again, but its check still matched the bridge's older message, so the retry went to the generic one, which found the dialogue closed. The check now matches the bridge's "the trade was refused" error. Faekregux's attempt at 08:24 failed to split one Gargoyle Eye from a stack, and the retry found its Shovel in the trade window, where the first attempt had put it. A give cut short before Give now presses the window's Cancel, which returns the offered items to the bags.
- The kill prior priced deaths with two means over the simulator's fights, 1.53 deaths and 0.28 leader deaths per won fight, which are raid-scale fights, so every hunt the simulator cannot fight (any enemy of level 50 or below) cost about a quarter of a leader's death per fight. With the chain's six fights, that left the Iksar Scale at 4.9 hours of cost against 1.6 of value. The prior now fits the deaths and the leader's death on the enemy's level and hit points like the chance, and a chain's fights are priced each at its own enemy's level. A reaver fight for group 1 now costs 0.01 leader deaths, and the reaver visit counts 0.26 hours of cost against 1.09 of value.

## Decisions to review

- A generated part's `minLevel` takes the median level of each zone it walks less three levels, for the walk's safety. The Shaman's hand-in in Emerald Jungle came out at 33, where the hand chain had 46.
- Two NPCs in Emerald Jungle are named Spirit Sentinel, and only one holds the Spear script. The bot finds NPCs by name, so the first Spear hand-in has to show which one the walk picks.
- The zones of the next band row count from two levels below it. The rows were chosen for their own levels, so those first visits two levels early carry more risk than the row's own zones.
- The measured crowd share counts as two hours of the model's (`CROWD_PRIOR_H`), so a zone shared for an hour still follows the model by two thirds.
- The member deaths per fight fit the prior's features poorly: a single log-linear rate still gives an easy enemy about 1.2 deaths a fight. They cost only their time, so the leader's death, which the fit separates well, sets most of a hunt's price.
