# 2026-11-09 (night): quest steps that hold, camps and gear by the game's own rules

## What happened

- The Paladin note chain stopped at the prisoner in West Freeport. The game showed why: Path To opened "That quest NPC is no longer available" without walking, because the client opens a quest NPC's dialogue only when the NPC is loaded near the party, and the prisoner stands in a jail cell 700 units from the zone-in. The bot also hailed the wrong one of two prisoners on one spawn point. Talks now walk to the spawned NPC with the client's ground navigation and hail that NPC. All four Paladins passed the step, three of them without help.
- A craft "failed" with an empty reason. The full reply showed a failed skill roll ("All 1 attempts failed."), which the bridge took for an error. A failed roll is now an attempt, and the quest step rolls until it succeeds, pulling one input per attempt and naming the input when the account runs out.
- The note chain counted a character as done only while it held the Sealed Note, which the next chain hands in for a Note, so two Paladins would have restarted the chain. Done items now follow the chains that consume a reward, and the keep list holds only open chains' inputs.
- A failure under older code is retried after the next deploy instead of waiting its two hours.
- Camps ended with "camp held" from the spawn list of the zone entry, which still showed placeholders the client's camp had killed. They read a fresh list now. Armor and jewelry camps wait until a whole group is 60, because the leveling model puts their value near zero, and the long-DoT crossover is queued behind the floor crossover with the new `after` field.
- The bag reserve keeps 20 units of food and of drink instead of a stack of every kind, and a bags alert closes once the reserve is back. A failing dashboard request shows its error instead of "Unexpected token '<'".
- Party buffs are supplied passively by each member's scribed class spells. No member lacks one at its level.
- Gear is valued by the client's formulas: weapons add the flat main-hand bonus and their procs, armor counts what it adds to the wearer's output (offense and haste for melee, the share of the measured mana pool for casters), and the formulas are in docs/client-internals.md.
- Tradeskills: caps, the success rule and a ladder planner (`tools/tradeskills.mjs`). The Warrior epic needs Tinkering for Rebreathers, best crafted at 148.

## Measured

- Model sensitivity at the best zone: 10% more party damage gives 5% more XP per hour at 20, 7.4% at 29 and 8.5% at 45, and 100 more tank AC at most 0.5%.
