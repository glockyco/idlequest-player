# 2026-11-10 (late night): gear trips by kill chance

## What happened

- The dashboard's Most wanted list showed nearly every target waiting for group 1. Three rules caused it:
  - Reach was a level margin, the leader plus 3, so 342 targets at levels 47 to 55 were group 1's alone and 273 at 56 and up were out of reach.
  - Each hunt took one enemy: group 1 camped the Elite Dragoon while 14 more of its targets in the same Plane of Hate waited.
  - A slot a hunt served blocked every other hunt for it.
  - The other groups were not idle: at 22:24, nine of them were camping their own targets. The list showed only the 15 most valuable targets, all in group 1's band.
- Gear acquisition step 2 went in (`docs/plans/gear-acquisition.md`).
  - Each group's kill chance against an enemy combines three kinds of fights: a model fitted to every simulated fight, the simulator's fights of the group's six for the pairs of highest expected value, and the group's own kills and escapes at its level.
  - The coordinator chooses by expected value, deaths included.
  - A trip takes a group to one zone for every wanted enemy there that pays.
  - A slot another hunt serves still takes a copy that comes sooner, for the hours until the better copy would come.
- Step 1 went in before it: every live zone's loot tables are drop sources, and `data/gear-picks.json` is gone. The raid kit and `simulate` went in with them, and the dashboard's deaths column counts again.
- The client's raid mode refuses an NPC at or below level 50 ("Select an NPC above level 50 or an already engaged enemy."), so the simulator prices only enemies above 50. For lower ones the model and the live record decide.

- A won fight cost 0.51 deaths in the simulator and a lost one 5.13, of them 0.06 and 0.98 the leader's.
- The first passes sent out these trips:
  - Group 1 camped the Plane of Hate for two enemies, group 6 Frontier Mountains for nine, group 8 Kael for three and group 11 Dalnir for four.
  - Group 12, at 41 to 43, visited Velketor for three level-47 rares and the Eastern Wastes for two named enemies.
  - Group 6's camp killed about a goblin a minute on its nine enemies' points.
  - The fight record took 119 rows in its first six minutes.
- Loading every live zone's drops takes 1.6 s once per collector process: 63,917 rows from 4,083 enemies in 121 zones.

## Mistakes

- The first trip value was a rate times the trip's length. The Disciple of Innoruuk's 7 points at a 640 s respawn and 25% drops give a Teir`Dal Sai every six minutes, so group 1's 30-minute camp was valued at 73.4 hours. Now a trip fills each slot at most once, which ran from the next pass on.
- The first kill-odds run spent fights on 11 enemies at level 50 or below, which the client refused. The tool now leaves them out.

## Decisions to review

- The model counts as one fight, so the simulator's four fights per pair and a few live fights outweigh it.
- A pair without simulated fights takes its deaths per fight from the model's means for won and lost fights.
- The guard's exemption for planned targets (step 3) waits: its level rule fires at 8 levels above the leader, where the model gives 0.09.
