# 2026-11-11 (evening): hidden tabs stop the fleet, and Fable 5.2 becomes the main model

## What happened

- omp restarted at 19:51 and took the collector and the browser relay with it, because both ran inside it. The tabs kept playing by themselves.
- At 19:59:58 a new session started the relay again to bring the collector back. From that second every game tab reported `hidden`. The likely cause, not verified: the earlier sessions had driven each tab through the relay, the browser emulated focus for those tabs, and the new relay connection dropped that. The browser then throttled the pages' timers, and since the client simulates combat in the page, fights stopped ending. Ten of the twelve groups made no kill for 18 to 23 minutes, a raid and a gear task failed at "stop took longer than 75 s", and Thalgrimm's page stayed without the bridge after its memory reload.
- The stall alerts fired at 20:06. The session read them at 20:18, found `vis` 0 in the samples and asked the browser at 20:21:17 to emulate focus in every game tab (`Emulation.setFocusEmulationEnabled`). All twelve groups killed again within two minutes, and Thalgrimm's page came back.
- The collector now asks for that focus in every game tab it reads, again for each new session and every minute (`src/cdp.mjs`), so the tabs no longer depend on a session that drives them.
- Sessions in this repository start on Claude Fable 5.2 at high effort (`.omp/config.yml`), with Opus 5.5 as the fallback and as the reviewer's model.
- `AGENTS.md` became a short brief that makes the agent the codebase's owner, and the operating text moved to `docs/runbook.md`. The repository is on GitHub as a private repository, and pushing is part of finishing work.
- The first potion raid ran at 20:23, on Lodizal in the Iceclad Ocean, led by Tagujazolu with 71 members. The raid stood 5,580 units from Lodizal after a minute, no fight began, and the task ended it after 55 s with everyone standing. The potion is spent.
- After that raid group 1 was labelled as fighting with AutoLevel off from 20:26 to 20:37, and again from 20:47 to 20:53. Each time a raid task had held AutoLevel at its "stop" step, the fight did not end within 70 s, the task failed, and the page stayed in combat with AutoLevel off. The label rules call that a fight, so no remedy started AutoLevel. `iq.leaveFight` in the page cleared it both times, and the bot had the party hunting again within a minute.
- The coordinator queues that raid again every pass, because the hourly item survey still lists the potion, and each such task can stick group 1 again.
- Play now runs from `tools/play.sh` in a Tern tab named "IdleQuest play": the browser relay and the collector in the foreground, until Ctrl-C, a closed tab or a reboot. Nothing restarts it. The switch from the omp service took the tabs out of focus for 50 s.
- The goal is `docs/goal.md`, about 170 words where the last one had 1,061, and a new session started with it.

## Measured

- Hidden, a page took about 4 samples a minute where it takes 60 (Pivorandel, 20:00 to 20:09).
- The fleet lost about 21 minutes of play in every group, from 19:59:58 to 20:21.

## Decisions to review

- Sessions no longer start or stop play. They leave stopped play alone and restart the collector only for a change to its code.
- The repository on GitHub is private, because it holds data extracted from the game client and the account's character names.
- Group 1's stuck fight was cleared by hand twice. The three raid defects are a step in the vault note and have no fix yet, so it can recur until the item survey drops the potion.
- Three alerts from before the restart stayed unhandled: the leader death of 19:10, the Blood Riven Axe errand that ended crowded in Kithicor, and the hunt that failed at "stop" at 19:27.
