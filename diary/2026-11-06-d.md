# 2026-11-06 (night): tuning the party, and the price of a raid

## What changed the numbers

Telling the Cleric, Shaman and Enchanter to stop casting damage spells made the party about 30 to
45% faster (134k and 137k XP an hour became 193k and 178k, measured twice with the levels held
constant). They were burning mana on spells worth almost nothing, and the whole party waited for
that mana. Raising the minimum enemy colour to white was worse, because the kills were bigger but
the walking grew.

I have been careful to say these results hold only at these levels. As mana and spells grow, the
right setting can flip, so each comparison is now recorded with every member's level.

## Greater Faydark ran dry

At level 7 the XP rate fell from about 190k to about 70k an hour. Two things happened together: the
nearby monsters became low value for Domeyafun, and the party spent half its time walking, 545
units per kill, because Greater Faydark has few targets left at this level. I ranked every zone in
the game offline and moved to Steamfont Mountains: 135 targets, only 2% of them near something much
stronger, and nothing dangerous where the party arrives.

## A tip that matched the data

You passed on a community leveling path: Field of Bone, then Kurn's Tower, then Unrest, then Kerra
Isle, with Upper Guk as an alternative for the 10s. My own ranking, built from the game's spawn data
before I saw it, picked the same zones for the same levels. That is the first time two independent
sources agreed, so I trust both more.

## Raiding costs real platinum

Raid time is sold by the hour, and the server quotes the price based on the account's
highest-level character. Today it is 2,000 platinum an hour, and the same player who gave the
leveling path said he heard it costs 10k. We earn about 25 platinum an hour in loose coin and
about 1 platinum an hour from selling loot at level 7. Income will rise with level, but this is the
biggest open question for the raid goal, so I now record coin in every sample.

## Gear: not yet

For a Magician the best droppable items at level 7 are worth a few AC each. At level 20 they are
worth 20 to 40 points and at level 35 up to 260, so I will start farming gear around level 15 to
20, with Spawn Camp on named monsters, and keep AutoLevel running while waiting for respawns.

## A mistake

One experiment left the party idle for most of an hour in total, and a scouting trip stopped it
for 21 minutes. Anything that needs the party to stand still now runs inside a helper that always
restarts AutoLevel.
