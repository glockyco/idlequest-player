# 2026-11-09 (early morning): selling, server cleanups, NPC waits and Warrior W5

## What happened

- The server's Auto-Sell emptied full bags of everything it does not protect, and its gear protection keeps only items scoring 50 or more. It sold Fine Steel weapons the gear plan was about to move, between the survey and the pulls. The bot now keeps Auto-Sell off on every character it plays, also inside tasks, and its own cleanup sells unwanted items at any price, cheapest first, while it keeps the gear plan's items, quest items and items with an effect.
- Tykar Renlin never spawned for the Paladin note chain, because Lozani, a level 30 East Freeport NPC, stands on the only spawn point Tykar shares with six others, and a point rolls again only when its occupant dies. NPC waits now get their spawn points from the content snapshot and end at once while another living NPC holds every point.
- A container from a quest fits only an empty general slot, and every character fills all eight with bags. The bot now moves its emptiest bag to the personal bank, which works away from a banker, places the container, and brings the bag back once a slot is free.
- A drop errand in a zone that other players crowd now ends after a crowded minute instead of idling until its deadline. Errands also lost what they hunted for: a wanted drop was not on the bot's keep list, so group 2 sold the Elf-hide Gloves two seconds after its errand looted them, and group 10 a Runed Circlet. The gear plan now keeps every wanted drop.
- The server's Delete Clutter deletes the NO DROP items it cannot sell when bags are full, wanted gear included, but it skips bags marked No Declutter. Each bot now marks its largest bag No Declutter and No Sell and keeps the wanted NO DROP pieces in it, such as the Powers of Fire and Water, Lord Gimblox's Signet Ring, the Mastery pages and the Warrior totems. What the coordinator's hunts, errands and quest chains are for is never sold either, and the gear plan keeps every drop that would be an upgrade for someone.
- Warrior W5 runs as a quest: all five Warriors held the Totem of the Freezing War and the account held two Hearts of Frost. Pieces for a chain are now counted by the members who still need it, and a chain's pulls never take a copy another needer carries, after Tagujazolu's task took Embrogg's heart while Embrogg walked to Tenal.
- Recovery between fights is the largest loss outside tasks. The client's wait needs every mana user at a full bar, and a bar refills a fixed share per tick, so the share of the bar a fight spends sets the wait. An experiment now runs the six Necromancer groups with and without their companions' DoTs.
- Smaller fixes: the gear plan reads lore items from `loregroup`, a stopped task releases its tab at once, a merchant is selected again for every purchase, an errand's page call gets the time its travel takes, a task past 90 minutes is stopped by the coordinator, research pages named "Pg." are never sold as single items, and the level triggers that asked for manual zone checks are gone, because the coordinator ranks zones by measured rates.
