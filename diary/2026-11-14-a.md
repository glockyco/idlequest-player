## Decisions to review

- An epic is a goal with a time budget, not a gear value. Piece hunts take up to 15% of a group's hour on top of the gear trips' share (`huntShare` in `tools/lib/coordinator.mjs`).
- A member's full bags are emptied only when a give is refused, and only of gear the plan has no use for. The gear goes to the leader, whose bag reserve sells or discards it.
- A stuck approach gets its remedy after 20 s instead of 60 s. Of the approaches that stood for 20 s in the samples of groups 9 and 10, about one in five went on by itself before 60 s. It now gets the remedy too, which skips the target for ten minutes.
- A companion's pet stays until its owner's class gets a new summon by the class spell table. The table also lists summons that only drop, so a pet is replaced at those levels even when the owner has not got the spell.
- A wait that repeats without a known time doubles up to eight hours. A quest NPC that comes back earlier is found late.
- A potion refused for a missing spawn is held for seven days.
- The collector asks for a relay restart when one tab stays detached for two minutes. The restart detaches every tab for a few seconds.
- `heapShare` stays at 0.3. The machine had 61 MB of unused memory and 5.8 of 6.1 GB of swap in use, and the 51 new tab processes of the 18.5 hours cost about 0.7% of the time.
- The vault's clone on this machine had diverged from its remote, with a working tree equal to the remote's. It was moved onto the remote with `git reset --mixed`, which changed no file.
- A party goes back to its rotation at the first tick after a trip ended. It no longer fights in the trip's zone while it waits, which had also brought 188 kills in the 18.5 hours.
- An escape of a leader below a group's level counts half for each level between (`ESCAPE_DECAY` in `tools/lib/fight-record.mjs`). Trips to enemies the groups lost to a level or two earlier become rarer, also where the group would now win.

## Third part: heals and a weapon proc

### Why a tank dies beside a Cleric with full mana

- Vexmorrath died 65 times between 18:23 on 2026-11-11 and 08:30 on 2026-11-14. In 35 of those the Cleric landed under 100 HP on him in his last 45 s.
- The Cleric healed the leader instead in 2 of the 65, and a pet for 800 HP or more in 7 of the 35.
- The Cleric was not out of mana. In the three deaths traced it had 1,871 to 1,935 of about 2,080 mana five seconds before the death. In two of them the tank fell from over 2,300 HP to under 1,000 within three seconds and stood below a fifth of his HP for 8 and 14 s, while the Cleric cast Minor Healing. Minor Healing is among the Cleric's casts in 16 of the 35.
- Small heals are rare otherwise: 23 of the 428 heal casts by the account's Clerics between 09:00 and 10:00 were Minor Healing, Light Healing or Healing.
- The rule is in the client (`Lq` in its main bundle, written down in docs/client-internals.md, "How a healer picks its heal"). A heal is judged by the target's HP when it lands. For a target that would be dead by then, the shortest cast wins, and a Cleric's shortest heal is Minor Healing: 10 HP in a second.
- `f1df83c` adds the bot setting `healMinHp`, which leaves the heals below that amount out for a caster that has a larger one. With 300 a Cleric at 57 keeps Remedy (438 HP in 3 s) as its fastest heal, a Paladin Light of Life, a Druid and a Shaman Chloroblast, a Ranger Greater Healing. Necromancers and Shadow Knights keep their small heals, because they have no larger one. It is 0 until an experiment sets it.

### Half the heals go to pets

- Between 09:00 and 10:00 the 21 healers landed 396,364 HP of heals, and 191,033 of it, 48%, on pets. In group 7 the pets got 96,551 and the six characters 33,191.
- The client has a tactic for this, `healPets`, per companion and on by default. The bot keeps only `cure` off.
- Pets die 11.3 times an hour across the account with their heals on (209 in 18.5 hours), and the characters 6.3 times (116). Without their heals the pets will die more often, so the setting has to be measured as charm, cures and DoTs were.

### Decisions to review, sixth and seventh parts

- A quest reward counts only for the classes whose tree has a finite cost and no step the bot lacks, and the tree of the first such class stands for all of them.
- The gate compares the reward's value with 0.1 hours a step plus the model's cost of its pieces. The model's cost is a rough guide, so a reward needing a raid kill stays out until its value is far larger.
- A gear chain's holders come from the hourly survey, so for up to an hour after a reward is handed over the coordinator may send a character for it again.
