# 2026-11-14: audit of a day of play

The audit read the collector's log, the database, the coordinator's history and the twelve leaders' logs from the page deploy of 2026-11-13 12:57 to 2026-11-14 07:30 (18.5 hours), and repaired what the data showed.

## What the day showed

- Time by label over the twelve groups: fight 62.4%, recover 15.4%, approach 9.8%, task 8.7%, crowded idle 1.7%. A double XP event has run since 01:44 on 2026-11-14, and the groups earn 4.5 to 5.8M XP an hour in it.
- Gear trips: 338 visits, 169 with a kill (50%, against 45% the day before), 36.9 hours, 126 wanted items (3.4 an hour). 60 hunts ran to their time limit and took 22.2 hours, 56 of them with kills. Errands took 42.9 hours for 64 items.
- Gear moves: 217 of 732 succeeded. 415 were refused with "no inventory space for the replaced item" and 97 with "inventory is full". A member's bags fill with gear it no longer wears, and nothing emptied them.
- Epic chains: the generated chains ran the steps of an epic in the order of the tree walk, so a Paladin who held the items of a later branch was sent to the first one again. The piece hunts shared their 15% of the hour with the gear trips, which used it up, so no piece hunt ran.
- 34 quest tasks waited for Jaeil the Wretched, two minutes apart at first. His script removes him with `eq.depop()`, which starts no respawn timer, and his spawn point has been missing from The Hole's spawn list since a hand-in on 2026-11-13. In that hand-in's fight one hammer was lost: Logresaerk killed Jaeil the Insane at 03:26, the corpse's loot held the Soulbound Hammer, and no "You loot" line came in the 23 s before the task ended. It is the only such case in the history.
- Stuck approaches: 223 remedies in 18.5 hours, 198 of them in the five groups with a Bard. Each time the party stood for 60 s before the remedy ran. (The commit message of `5c5ad97` says 194, which is wrong.)
- Pets: 67 upkeeps dismissed companions' pets that the same summon then brought back.
- Group 9's tab answered "Debugger is not attached" from 07:35 to 08:06 although the relay listed it as attached. A relay restart brought it back.
- Tunare's potion raid failed twice. At 19:55 the task switched character while the party was in combat. At 02:33 the potion was refused after the task had bought an hour for 10,000 platinum: "The boss's authored spawn is unavailable: sql: no rows in result set".
- The mechanisms the last audit deployed work: the coin hand-in gave the books and 1,000 platinum to Rysva To-Biath (Fisugrond 14:46, Hargrimol 15:53), and a refused escape logged "escape failed" once.

## What changed

- `862049b` `tools/fights.mjs` reads the logs as streams. Same output on a 20 MB fixture, peak memory 496 MB before and 176 MB after. It also takes `--last` and `--since`.
- `0fa4b70` Generated chains are split by dependency: one part per item, with the parts it needs as its only prerequisites. A part is done when its item is held or was used by a later part. A hand-kept chain takes precedence, and the generated part is cut down to the steps the hand-kept chain does not cover. The bot resumes a part at the step whose items the member holds and repeats a step that calls an NPC.
- `f276c78` Piece hunts have their own 15% of a group's hour, counted apart from gear trips. Six hunts that were bound to group 1 now go to any group at 46 to 60. Three pieces point at their generated chain parts.
- `fdf2ba8` The gear pass lists each member's spare gear, which is gear the plan gives it no use for. When a give is refused for space, the bot pulls that spare gear to the leader and gives again. It does the same before a quest switch. The bag reserve no longer runs for a member.
- `a74fefa` A wait that repeats with the same text and no known time doubles, up to eight hours.
- `e2b3064` A tab without a bot counts as busy when a task cannot be queued.
- `f9500a4` The raid task stops the fight before it switches character. A potion the server refuses for a missing spawn is held for seven days. The kill time counts from the engagement.
- `e11f3dc` When a tab answers "Debugger is not attached" for two minutes, the collector asks `tools/play.sh` to restart its relay.
- `5c5ad97` An approach is stuck after 20 s without movement, counted from its start. In the samples of four groups, three of four approaches that stood for more than 5 s stood until the remedy.
- `e7ccd68` A companion's pet is replaced only when its owner's class got a new summon since the last replacement. The level of the last replacement is kept in the tab's sessionStorage.
- `3ead474` The dashboard says "The last group, G9, reaches 60 in about ...", and Next retries leaves out quests of chains that no longer exist.
- `b5b3ee6` Each game tab is titled after its group and leader, such as "G7 Gygekrian | IdleQuest".
- `e2287a1` A party that an errand or a task took out of its rotation goes back at the first tick after it ended. Before, it waited 60 s from its arrival, and AutoLevel fought in the trip's zone meanwhile: 369 returns in the 18.5 hours with 109 fights and 7 deaths, 3.1 hours in all.
- `f7684ea` The fight record counts an escape of a leader below the group's level at half for each level between. Before, each level a group gained kept the kills and dropped the escapes. Captain Bvellos had one kill and 27 escapes against leaders of 54 to 56, and for a leader of 57 the record read one kill and no escape.

## Decisions to review

- An epic is a goal with a time budget, not a gear value. Piece hunts take up to 15% of a group's hour on top of the gear trips' share (`huntShare` in `tools/lib/coordinator.mjs`).
- A member's full bags are emptied only when a give is refused, and only of gear the plan has no use for. The gear goes to the leader, whose bag reserve sells or discards it.
- A stuck approach gets its remedy after 20 s instead of 60 s. Of the approaches that stood for 20 s in the samples of groups 9 and 10, about one in five went on by itself before 60 s. It now gets the remedy too, which skips the target for ten minutes.
- A companion's pet stays until its owner's class gets a new summon by the class spell table. The table also lists summons that only drop, so a pet is replaced at those levels even when the owner has not got the spell.
- A wait that repeats without a known time doubles up to eight hours. A quest NPC that comes back earlier is found late.
- A potion refused for a missing spawn is held for seven days.
- The collector asks for a relay restart when one tab stays detached for two minutes. The restart detaches every tab for a few seconds.
- `heapShare` stays at 0.3. The machine had 61 MB of unused memory and 5.8 of 6.1 GB of swap in use, and the 51 new tab processes of the 18.5 hours cost about 0.7% of the time.
- The vault's clone on this machine had diverged from its remote, with a working tree equal to the remote's. It was moved onto the remote with `git reset --mixed`, which changed no file.
- A party goes back to its rotation at the first tick after a trip ended. It no longer fights in the trip's zone while it waits, which had also brought 188 kills in the 18.5 hours.
- An escape of a leader below a group's level counts half for each level between (`ESCAPE_DECAY` in `tools/lib/fight-record.mjs`). Trips to enemies the groups lost to a level or two earlier become rarer, also where the group would now win.
