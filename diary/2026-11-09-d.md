# 2026-11-09 (evening): green pulls, full bags, and an inventory with one owner

## What happened

- Some groups made far less XP per hour than others. The largest cause was not zones or mana: eight groups pulled with minimum con Green, and green enemies pay nothing. The agreed Blue to Red limits had lived only in experiment settings stored in each tab, so the tabs opened after the machine restart had no target, and camps and errands left the minimum at Green. The bot now holds Blue to Red, cap 5, as its default.
- The White against Blue test had left four groups on White for 13 hours, because its entry left the experiment file two minutes before its end. iqd now returns the groups of an experiment removed early to its other arm and registers no job for it.
- `tools/analyze.mjs` ignored DoT ticks, the most common damage line, because the client writes them without a spell name. It now counts them for their caster.
- Bags filled in every group: items without a merchant price had no way out after the LORE and quest exemption of 07:48, and the gear plan kept every copy of every upgrade. After a review (`docs/plans/inventory.md`, approved), the bot owns the inventory. It keeps each item up to its demand and holds a reserve of 8 free slots with Auto Bank, sales and discards. The gear plan sends copy counts and the copies its moves take. The server's Auto-Sell and Delete Clutter stay off, and the protected bag and the sale tiers are gone.
- The first version left items on the cursor when the client refused a destroy while the moved item was still updating. The destroy now repeats, finishes later, and a held item that cannot go back into full bags leaves by its fate.
- AutoLevel limits take an optional floor below the leader (`levelsBelow`), for a crossover at 24 to 33.
- The dashboard's first stage follows the approved plan: what needs a person, goal bars, one row per group and the coordinator by name.
- The Paladin chain's craft step works now (Edible Goo crafted), and the chain stops at the prisoner in West Freeport, who stands in a jail cell.
