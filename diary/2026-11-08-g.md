# 2026-11-08 (evening): directed hunts, Power of the Elements and a corrected wisp count

## What happened

- The goal changed. Every roster character goes to level 60, AA ranks and the epics each character should carry are part of the end state, raids start when they pay, and the implementation has to improve with the results, measured by manual interventions and stalls per group-hour.
- AutoLevel never pulls gray enemies, so the bot gained a hunt task. It lets the fight end, switches to the character who must loot a NO DROP piece, brings the rest of the group along, makes bag room, and fights the named enemies through the client's Enemy Targets action. The first run left Domeyafun alone with full bags while the others stood idle. That run is the reason the task now gathers the group, counts the leader as a member and frees bag space first.
- Domeyafun took Power of Wind in Mistmoore and Power of Earth in the Greater Faydark, then Walnan in Butcherblock took all four Powers in one trade and gave Power of the Elements. The trade code would have sold that reward, because it sold any cursor item that was not part of the trade. Rewards now go into the bags.
- Torn Page of Magi`kot pg. 1 drops from enraged dread wolves that exist only at night in Kithicor. The SDK now reads the game clock (`iq.gameHour()`, day is 5 to 20, a game day lasts 40 minutes), and `tools/drops.mjs` names night-only and day-only spawns. The hunt at nightfall took the page on the second wolf. No ghoul for page 3 was up in Lower Guk.
- The wisp pilot first looked like a loss. `tools/loot.mjs` gave a loot line to its kill only within 3 s, but half the loot lines arrive 3 to 4 s after the kill. With the client's 15 s loot deadline, groups 3, 6 and 8 at levels 11 to 13 got 12 Greater Lightstones and 19 Lightstones from 108 wisps in 39 minutes each, about 3.0M XP per group-hour at Smith Tv`ysa, against 1.5M of kill XP given up. Wisp farming is now lever 3 of the leveling plan, and the three groups farm again.
