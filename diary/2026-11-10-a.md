# 2026-11-10: one owner for every fault

## What happened

- The server was slow and restarted several times, and each fault had grown its own fix in a different place. The fault-handling plan (docs/plans/fault-handling.md) gave each fault one owner and one cure, and all six of its steps are built.
- AutoLevel now starts in one place, the remedy table, by the cause the page shows for each idle second: the client's own pause lines, a failed zone load, a target another player engaged, the bot's holds, and `unknown` for a stop nothing explains. A failed zone load is answered with the client's own Try Again.
- One pure page supervisor (tools/lib/page.mjs) keeps each page signed in, with the current bridge and bot, within its memory and without a known client fault. It signs in at most six tabs per half minute, because twelve sign-ins at once saw refusals, and it gives a page the client's minute to resume a session first. It signed tabs in after failed campings three times this afternoon, each after 60 s at the login form.
- Client defects live in one section of the bridge with a count of how often each acted, and tools/check-client.mjs looks for each one's anchor and defect in a new release. The loot preview shim had been calling back at 1.5 s on every kill, although a healthy preview takes 3 s. It now waits 3.5 s, which AutoLevel's 5 s pause after a kill hides.
- Read-only requests go through one query that asks a slow server three times and refuses anything that could act, such as the daily boss list with a boss key, which summons the boss.
- Samples name their character, so a switch no longer shows as -22M or +32M XP per hour.
- Verifying these turned up older defects. The rotation rule counted minutes signed out as a zone that earned nothing. A quest's switch read an empty character list after a camping the server could not confirm and failed with "no account character". A failed zone move woke a script on every try. The bag reserve sold before it discarded, so ten leaders carried 28 to 41 worthless quest drops, about two thirds of their bags, and discards then left items on the cursor while the server had not confirmed the move, which cost Muvelemoru a Dusty Ransacker's Pack. Each is fixed: play time for the rate, a switch that waits for the page, a move backoff, discards first, and no pass and no fight while the bot's own discard waits on the server.
- The coordinator re-ran every failed task after every deploy. G9's Bard epic ran five times in 42 minutes and failed each time. A failure that repeats the one before it at the same step now waits its retry interval.
- The Progress feed and the group details show item icons with the game's item window, and gear rows show the old and the new item.

## Measured

- Idle in the first 51 group-minutes after the single AutoLevel owner: 1.4%.
- Failed campings: 10 of about 370 switches, six of them between 15:04 and 15:57 while the server lagged.
- Bard epic: done for Kimorili and Muvelemoru, failed at "talk to Baenar Swiftsong" eight times for four groups, because the `[request]` option shows only sometimes.
- After the discard order changed, the leaders destroyed about 50 worthless items within five minutes, with no refused destroy once a pass waited for a confirmed inventory.
