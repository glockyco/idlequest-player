# 2026-11-07 (early afternoon): stepping back from the probes

Domeyafun is level 22, Tagujazolu 22 and the four others 20 and 21. The party hunts in the Permafrost Caverns, and the bot rotates to the City of Guk when Permafrost's reachable spawns run out.

## What went wrong

For two hours I ran 5- and 10-minute A/B blocks on Domeyafun's nukes, the Cleric's Root, the Enchanter's cures and skipping shamans. Two blocks with the same setting differ by up to 15%, and those changes were worth a few percent, so most blocks could not decide anything. Meanwhile the party leveled from 19 to 22 without anyone asking whether Guk still fit, and it ran every block in the same zone.

## What the numbers say instead

- The zone is the biggest lever. At level 22 the band of enemies 20 to 25 holds 85 targets in Permafrost at a 1.44 multiplier, and 53 in Guk at 1.19, spread further apart. The first ten minutes back in Permafrost earned about 1.95M XP an hour, against about 1.6M in Guk at 21.
- Permafrost ran dry at level 19 because AutoLevel only pulls enemies it can reach, and spawns return after about ten minutes. The bot now cycles between the zones in its `rotation` setting when "No available enemies" persists or the last ten minutes earned under 60% of the visit's best.
- Fighting is 60% of the time. Out of combat everyone refills 2.5% of their bar every 0.6 seconds (wiki, "Companions"), so a full bar takes about 24 seconds and recovery gear changes little.
- Ugfeu sings only Brusco's Boastful Bellow, but that is fine. Party members supply their learned class buffs passively, bard songs included, so Cassindra's Chant of Clarity, Anthem de Arms and Selo's Accelerando apply without being sung.
- Damage at 20-21: the two pets 42%, the Bard 15%, Domeyafun's nukes 14%, the Warrior 12.5%, the Cleric 11%, the Shaman 6%. A weapon helps only by its owner's share.
- The two 2H swords the bot sold were worth little. Bronze (9/47) and Fine Steel (12/43) beat the Warrior's 8/37 axe by at most a quarter, on his main hand alone. Real upgrades are drops, such as the Bone Bladed Claymore (17/45) in Befallen and the Blackened Alloy Bastard Sword (24/39) in Runnyeye.

## Changes

- The bot skips enemy types named in `avoidNames` as targets. In Guk, "shaman|idealist" kept the Root casters off the list, and root waits had cost a tenth of the time there.
- The bot restarts AutoLevel 8 seconds after a route recovery that finds nothing. Every recovery that worked engaged within 2 seconds.
- Zone rotation as above, through the same zone-change request the Travel window sends.
- The leveling plan now ranks levers by expected effect, and a dedicated A/B test needs an expected effect above the noise.

## Decisions to review

- Rotation is Permafrost and Guk. A third zone (Runnyeye or Solusek's Eye) waits for the zone research.
- Root casters stay skipped in Guk without another A/B block. The cause (25 to 45 seconds of standing per root) is visible in the analyzer directly.
