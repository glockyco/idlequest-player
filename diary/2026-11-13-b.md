# 2026-11-13: why nobody looked at the bosses that were due

The dashboard showed Lord Nagafen "down, back Fri Nov 13 10:46 due" and Talendor "back 15:08 due" at 18:55, both last seen 30 hours before, and "No look is due". This entry is the audit of the watch that led there, and its repair.

## What was wrong

- A look went only to a boss the plan would raid at once. A boss outside the goal counted only inside paid raid time or while fewer than two hours of raid time ran in the last 24 (`OPEN_BUY.hoursADay`). Three hours had been bought the evening before, two of them for lost raids on Cazic-Thule, so from 05:29 to 22:27 no boss outside the goal could get a look. The account held 122,992 platinum, twelve hours of raid time. The limit also contradicted the project's note that raid time cost is no constraint.
- The two bosses were not due at all. The server restarted at 21:15 on 2026-11-12 and put bosses up ahead of their timers: six of the seven the tabs saw within the hour stood one to two days early (Lady Vox, Phinigel Autropos, Trakanon, Innoruuk, Cazic-Thule, Lord Yelinak), and Venril Sathir kept his time. Lord Nagafen, Talendor, Gorenaire and Faydedar kept the respawn times of the looks at noon, in zones where no group hunts, and nobody looked that evening. The four looks of today found all four dead again: other players killed them in the hours after the restart.
- The board said "down ... due" for a respawn time that had passed, which reads as a fact. Nobody had looked, so nothing was known.
- The "Killed" column was the respawn time less the content's respawn. The server draws each respawn inside the spawn point's variance (`variance` in `spawn2`). Our own raids of 2026-11-12 show it: Lady Vox killed at 22:13, back 60.4 hours later, Innoruuk killed at 22:47, back in 66.3, Trakanon killed at 22:15, back in 78.6, with 72 hours and 12, 21.6 and 7.2 hours of variance in the content. The board showed Lady Vox killed at 10:38, twelve hours early, and "how long it stood" inherited the error.
- One look went out per pass, so several due bosses took a pass each, five minutes apart.

## What changed

Two commits, collector code only, live since 19:17: `ad2c6d8` and `5fc57a2`. 432 tests.

- A look goes to every watched boss the raid kills in the simulator, whether or not an hour can be bought (`nextLooks`, `could`). A boss that stands and the plan would not raid is looked at every two hours, which measures how long it stands.
- An hour for an open-world boss outside the goal is bought when the kills are worth an hour of a group's progress and the account's coin pays that hour and one more for the summons (`OPEN_BUY`, `canBuyHour`). The count of hours a day is gone. The boss pass reads the coin only while such a boss stood at its last sighting.
- A boss seen alive more than ten minutes ahead of the respawn time on record is evidence that the server reset the spawns, and every older sighting of a boss that was down is void (`spawnReset`). Those bosses get a look at once and read "not known" on the board.
- A respawn time that has passed reads "may be up since ..., with no look since". The summary counts those bosses.
- A kill is a window: one respawn before the respawn time, give or take the variance, narrowed to after the boss was last seen alive and before it was first seen down (`killWindow`, `lastAt` in `bossLog`). Phinigel Autropos, back at 10:39 today, died between 13:55 and 15:02, so he stood 3.3 to 4.4 hours. That is the only measurement of how long a boss stands.
- A pass sends up to four looks, each with a group of its own.
- The board names what a look waits for when every group is on a task, how many more looks are due, and the buying rule with the account's coin.

## Found and not repaired

- The look's result names the next spawn by its clock time only ("next spawn at 19:11" for a respawn two days on). It is page code, and the board shows the full time, so it waits for the next deploy.
- A raid on a summon reads no spawn list, so it does not see whether the same boss stands in the open world of its zone. Whether the list tells a private copy from the open-world boss is not known.
- Group 1 alone kills Lord Nagafen, Lady Vox, Faydedar and Phinigel Autropos in four simulated fights of four, which would need no raid time. The plan sends raids only, because the simulator fights a boss without its company (docs/plans/raids.md).
- Whether the server lets a raid fight an open-world boss inside the day after the account killed its summoned copy is not known. Lord Nagafen's summoned copy was killed twice within six hours (22:25 and 04:30), the second time by "Resume".
- A sighting is kept by the enemy's name alone (`bossesSeen`), so a boss with a namesake in another zone would lose its sighting to a group there. No group has been in such a zone.
- A raid takes every group off its hunt for two to four minutes, and a boss's worth counts its loot only. Phinigel Autropos at 0.1 hours is raided in a paid hour all the same.

## Decisions to review

- The limit of two open-world raid hours a day is gone, and the account's coin is the limit: an hour is bought while 20,000 platinum are there, for kills worth an hour of a group's progress. Six bosses outside the goal are worth that much and come back every three days, so this buys two hours a day at most, about what the sales bring with the summons' hour. To cap it again, add a count to `OPEN_BUY`.
- Looks no longer depend on the purchase. A boss that stands unraided costs a group a minute every two hours (`LOOK.standMs`).
- Ten minutes ahead of its respawn time counts as a reset (`RESET_EARLY_MS`). A false one costs one look per boss that is down.
- Four looks a pass (`LOOKS_A_PASS`).
