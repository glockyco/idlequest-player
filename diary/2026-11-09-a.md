# 2026-11-09 (night): merchants, crafting, AA catalog and steadier zones

## What happened

- The gear plan buys merchant goods. City merchants count as sources with a price, within 20 pp a pass in one city, and the gear task travels there, buys and gives. Four Bards now wear bought Hand Drums, and casters wear Violet Sr Apprentice Robes and Ursa Tresses. A Bard's instrument takes the off hand, because it scales every song of its family for the whole group, while an off-hand weapon adds only the Bard's own swings.
- Crafting works without a container: the bot buys a recipe's catalog ingredients into the Tradeskill Bank, crafts with the Advanced window's request and withdraws the result. Five Bog Juice attempts gave two. The Paladin Sealed Note chain now runs as a quest that buys Drom's Champagne, crafts Bog Juice and Edible Goo and trades them up to the Sealed Note. Every Paladin got the Pure Crystal from Jark.
- The AA catalog came from the server: 16 innate abilities, +2 to a stat or resist per rank, 72 ranks of 75M XP and 2,000 pp each. XP goes to levels until 60, through the client's own rule that pauses the AA share below a level.
- Zones are assigned from measured XP per hour. A group keeps its rotation while it stays and for ten minutes after a new one, and groups in an experiment are spread like the others, because three of them crowding Kurn's Tower lost 13 to 20% of their time.
- Fixes at their cause: cures stay off for every companion as kept state (Imorymu cast Cure Poison with no overrides), a leader left at 0 HP after a stalled zone change camps and re-enters, the bot presses Try Again when a zone fails to load, a give stows a leftover cursor item first, and a camp gets only what is left of its tab's hunt share.
- Community knowledge from the P99 wiki is in docs/plans/community.md, each claim marked against what this repository shows.

## Measured

- Zone rates for groups 2 to 12 at 11 to 14: Kurn's Tower, the Warrens and Befallen about 500k XP/h, Crushbone 286k. At 15 to 24 Kurn's Tower 783k against Najena 422k.
- Recovery takes 19 to 47% of the low groups' time. Sitting, starving and mana buffs are not the lever.
- Group 1 spent 60% of half an hour on epic camps before the hunt share counted running camps, and 9% after.
- White against Blue minimum con at 15 to 19: +2% XP per hour for White (-14% to +22%) over eight groups, so the groups keep Blue. The first block ran crowded.
