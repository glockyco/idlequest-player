# 2026-11-10 (evening): the casino, the first daily bosses and their loot

## What happened

- The casino's Items prize gives the account one boss potion per 24 hours from level 46, and the account had rolled none since group 1 reached 46. The coordinator now rolls 9 dice once a day. Its first roll won a Potion of Lodizal on the first die throw for 600 pp. A potion is LORE and stays with its winner: the server refused all five gives to Tagujazolu's party with "Casino boss potions cannot be given to another character, including on your account." The roll therefore goes to the character of 46 or more that rolled longest ago, a member as well as a leader. The bag reserve would have destroyed the potion, which has no price and is NO DROP, so it keeps the whole potion range.
- The daily list's two free summons had gone unused every day. The raid simulator, now able to read the account as surveyed, gave group 1's six a kill in all six fights against Phinigel Autropos, Lady Vox, Faydedar, Lord Nagafen and Venril Sathir, and none against Talendor, Gorenaire, Trakanon, Innoruuk or Cazic-Thule.
- Phinigel, with all 72 characters, ran into the first boss mechanic the bot did not handle: a swirlspine seahorse that joined the fight healed him for 10,000 about every 30 s, and four times the raid brought him to about 6,000 only to see him back near 15,000. Attacking the seahorse by hand killed it in about 70 s and Phinigel 10 s later, and the raid then killed the zone's own Phinigel as well, 404 s in all with all 71 recruits standing. The raid task now attacks an engaged enemy that heals its boss first.
- Raid kills are not looted by Auto Loot: their corpses wait in the party panel's RAID LOOT tab. Phinigel's two corpses held Blue Crystal Staff, Staff of Elemental Mastery: Water, Kedge Backbone and Robe of the Kedge, epic pieces for four classes, and the task would have left them to rot. Looting them showed the next fault: the bag reserve rated three of the pieces as discards, because epic pieces have no price and are NO DROP. Every item a quest script takes is now kept when the reserve would destroy it.
- Lady Vox died in 100 s with all 71 recruits standing. The task's loot step found every click refused: the duplicate lore Fusible Coral Ore from Phinigel was still on the cursor, and the window returns a held item only to a corpse of its own zone. The loot step now places or hands on a held item before it starts and counts an item as looted only once it left its corpse. Vox's five items were collected by hand after a walk back to Permafrost.
- An hour of raid time costs 8,000 pp with the highest character at 52, against 2,000 at 46.
