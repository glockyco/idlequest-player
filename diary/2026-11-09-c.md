# 2026-11-09 (afternoon): the first raids, and a root dispel that never ran

## What happened

- After a machine restart all twelve groups came back through `restoreGroup` in their own tabs. iqd now remembers crowded zones across tab restarts, ends a vanished tab's session once its leader plays in a new one, and leaves out crowding reports from zones our own groups share, because the zone choice counts our groups already.
- Group 1's leader reached 46. An hour of raid time costs 2,000 pp, and the daily list offers ten bosses, of which Cazic-Thule is the only goal boss.
- The first raid sent All Attack and nothing else. Raid members act only on orders, so the four casters did nothing for 15 minutes, the Bard fought alone and died, and the leader stood 12 minutes, then fought the fanatic alone and died twice. His raid deaths cost no XP, and a raid Cleric revived him on command. The bridge could not end the raid: it called a stand-in object whose every method returns false instead of the client's combat service. Ending it out of combat with the real service sent a StopCombat the server left unanswered, and only a reload cleared it.
- Reading the current release's raid code gave the model now in `docs/client-internals.md`. Only melee classes swing. Casters cast only for a heal mode, a nuke order, a maintained effect or a queued cast. The nuke modes "DPS" use only Necromancers, Wizards and Magicians, and the leader moves in a frame loop that a root stops.
- The raid task now sets the heal mode, walks the raid to the boss, attacks once and waits for the fight, gives the nuke order and keeps a slow and a resist debuff on the target. It revives a fallen leader, always ends raid mode and traces the leader every 5 s. The server took all 71 other characters as raid recruits while their groups kept playing them, and a fight with all 72 started.
- The fanatic roots the tank with Engulfing Roots, strips slows and debuffs with Nullify Magic and flees at about 20%. The root outlasted the fight, and the bot never dispelled it: its root dispel had found the client's effects module by minified export names that the current release renamed, so it had never fired in any group. The bridge now finds the effects registry and the root test by their source, and the client-surface audit covers both.
- Hunt, raid and gear tasks now end a running errand as finished, as quest tasks did, so a task no longer leaves an errand to fail with "the party left".
- The band rows for 26 to 30 hold six zones each, because six groups level through 26-27 and nine reach 28-30. Permafrost stays out of row 28 for its ice giants.
