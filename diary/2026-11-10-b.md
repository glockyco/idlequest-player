## What happened

- The server went down from about 16:15 to 16:55. Pages came back at character select without a connection, or with "Too many login attempts" after the supervisor's sign-ins. The supervisor now reloads a character select that has had no connection for 60 s, a refusal holds every sign-in of the fleet for 70 s, and a page outside the world shows its failed load, so AutoLevel's remedy table answers it with Try Again. All twelve tabs played again by about 17:02 without a manual step.
- The client asked a paused page to camp and rejoin. That pause is now its own cause, `clientRejoin`, answered by a rejoin.
- A new client release renamed the hail function's parameters, so the bridge found no hail. The pattern now matches any parameter names, and check-client passes on both saved releases.

The server takes him out of automated combat during Natasha Whitewater's scene, and the Enemy Targets list leaves such spawns out. Outside a raid the client's Auto Attack button attacks any selected spawn, so the kill step now starts that fight on him directly. It runs at the next Lord Bergurgle's Crown.

The bridge took the install millisecond as a page's session id, the supervisor installed four pages within two milliseconds, and iqd took each pair for one page relisted under a new target id and ended the other's session every cycle. Measured by the number of other groups there at levels 35 and up, it paid 2.2 to 2.5M XP an hour with one to three others, 2.61M with four, 2.21M with five, 1.95M with six and 1.69M with seven. The crowding model's factors fit that shape. The zone's rate alone did not: iqd rated it 3.0 to 3.6M from 0.3 to 0.6 weighted hours alone per level, minutes that follow the other groups' departure with every spawn point up. The rates alone now come from all of a zone's time, each shared second counted at the model's share.
- The coordinator valued a zone for the next group at its own share, which ignores what it takes from the groups already there, so six groups gathered in Solusek's Eye: a sixth there earned about as much for itself as a third in Kaesora but added about 0.8M XP an hour to the account against 2.1M. Zones are now valued by what the group adds to the account, and the first pass moved G5 to Kaesora.

The server's fix for its slowness came the same afternoon, so the gain is not the placement's alone. Recovery (18 to 32%) and walking (13 to 28%) are now the largest shares besides fighting.

Supplied mana regeneration comes from an Enchanter (5 to 6) and a Bard (5), 11 to 12 with both and 0 in the six groups with neither, and at 11 the bars refill only about a fifth faster.
