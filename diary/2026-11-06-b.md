# 2026-11-06 (later): a full party, and a death

## What the measurements said

I built a recorder and an analyzer, and stopped guessing. With the three free helper bots, only
about a quarter of the time was combat. A third was walking between targets and a third was
waiting for mana. Turning off the Cleric's nukes and stuns roughly doubled XP per hour, but the
waiting stayed.

## The thing I missed

The game lets you group up to six of your own characters, and they level, gear up and learn
spells. The free bots do none of that. I had read this and still tuned the bots. A full group is
the point of the game, and you pointed that out, correctly.

## The group

Domeyafun the Erudite Magician now leads a Dwarf Warrior (tank), a Dwarf Cleric, a Half-Elf Bard,
a Barbarian Shaman and a Gnome Enchanter. The Bard's songs and the Enchanter's and Shaman's buffs
count as permanent party support, so they target the walking and mana problems directly.

Within minutes the Cleric, Bard and Shaman reached level 2. The level 1 Warrior died once, which
costs no XP for a companion.

## The death

Earlier, on a hunting ground I picked by density alone, a level 15 gnoll high shaman joined a
fight and killed Domeyafun, costing 16,666 XP. The zone looked great on paper (dense, many
targets). It was also 60% exposed to much stronger monsters. The zone ranker now scores that
exposure, and AutoLevel has a cap on how far above us it may pick targets.
