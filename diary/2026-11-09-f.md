# 2026-11-09 (late): camps by value, rests by the slowest bar

## What happened

- A camp for a weapon now runs below 60 only when the piece's expected value beats the XP the camp costs: the taker's output gain times its share of the party's damage, the XP elasticity at its level and the hours until it outlevels the piece, against the camp's expected hours. None passed at the first gear pass, so weapon drops come through errands in normal hunting and camps wait for 60.
- Worn effects and spell foci count in the gear value. No item in the content uses flat spell damage, extra skill damage or heal amount, and the eight damage shield items give 2 to 12 points.
- A stat-less Silver Earring kept its slot on five characters, because the earrings within reach add only AC and HP, which the leveling model values at almost nothing. The dashboard now says so instead of calling every slot the best available.
- AutoLevel pulls only when every member is at full HP and every mana class at full mana, with no setting, and the refill between fights is 2.5% of the maximum per 600 ms, up to twice as fast with mana regeneration 50. So a group rests as long as its slowest bar needs. The gear value counts mana regeneration, item mana and a mana cost focus by the group time they save on the member the party waits for, instead of a flat share of every caster's own output.
- I first read the Bard instruments wrong, and the game showed it: an instrument in the bags does reach the party supply, and Chant of Battle is not supplied in any group. The supply takes strings while anyone is below full HP or mana, percussion once everyone is full and brass in a fight, and below 42 only percussion pays, through Selo's Accelerando on the walk to the next pull. The gear plan now values a Bard's percussion instrument by the walking time it saves and moves it into the bags, where it costs no hand. Ugfeu had none.
- Six Magician and Enchanter epic pieces had no source in the opportunities, so no group looked for them. They are errands now, and group 1 took the Shining Metallic Robes errand in Old Guk at the next pass.
- Two finished experiments still showed as open jobs on the dashboard. Their results were recorded hours ago, and the jobs are closed.
- The gear task had failed to buy Ugfeu's Hand Drum three times. A probe in group 6's tab showed three causes: a merchant left selected from an earlier visit passed for an arrival, the PURCHASE button stays disabled for a moment after a consider, and the window's own consider of its first item replaced the asked one, so a Long Sword was bought instead. The buy now waits for the open window and for the asked item on screen. Ugfeu got his drum, and group 1's run speed went from 113 to 161.
- Moves pulling from Tagujazolu failed every pass: Inventory Search lists only items inside bags, and his were loose in a general slot. Such items no longer count as free copies.
- Wanted drops are now ranked by enemy, as a Most wanted list on the dashboard, with each enemy's gear gain per hour of hunting it: each item once, at its best taker. A first version summed every taker and showed a ring at 178% an hour. Each free group then takes the target whose worth in XP beats its cost most, an errand in its rotation or a hunt trip outside it, and the list says why a target is not hunted.
